About the League

Tennis, Friendship & Fun — every month.

A group of 12–15 tennis lovers who come together every month for a fun yet competitive morning of doubles. No fixed partnerships, no favourites — an AI-based team generator randomly creates the pairings before each edition, keeping things fresh and fair every single time.

The entire event wraps up in just 2½ hours: four doubles teams, a round-robin format, then a final between the top two. Competitive enough to matter, social enough to enjoy. And when the racquets go down, chai and breakfast bring everyone together.

Conceived and run by Rahul Gupta — who books the courts, coordinates the players, and quietly makes sure the whole thing happens every month.

How It Works

Three steps, every month.

🤖

AI Picks the Teams

No favourites, no debates. An AI-based team generator randomly creates doubles pairings — everyone gets a different partner each time.

🎾

Round Robin + Final

Four teams compete in a round-robin format. The top two meet in the final. The race to qualify isn't decided until the very last match.

☕

Chai & Breakfast

Every edition ends the same way — the whole group together over chai and breakfast, replaying the winners and debating the line calls.
