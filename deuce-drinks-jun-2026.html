How It Played Out

The Morning
of June 28

The first serve was struck at 6:30 AM, with everyone secretly hoping the rain would wait just a little longer. Thankfully, it did.

The enthusiasm was fantastic. Every player arrived on time, and a few passionate members even travelled long distances from Hadapsar and Hinjewadi to be part of the morning. A big thank you to L.J. Tennis Academy, Sanghavi for preparing the courts and supporting the event.

How the Teams Were Made

As always, the AI team generator did its job — randomly pairing up the doubles partnerships so there are no favourites, no negotiations, and no awkward politics. Just let AI decide and let the racquets do the talking.

"The AI certainly did its job well — every match was closely contested, with long rallies and exciting points."

A Race to the Final

Four doubles teams competed in a round-robin format. The race to the final wasn't decided until the very last round-robin match — which is exactly what the format is designed to do. Every match mattered.

When the dust settled, Abhinav and Chandan and Ivan and Ajinkya earned their places in the final. The final was an excellent display of tennis from both pairs — and Abhinav and Chandan took the title.

Chai, Breakfast & the Usual Debates

No social tennis tournament is complete without the classic "That ball was definitely in!" debates, a few spectacular winners, a couple of unexpected misses, plenty of laughter — and chai and breakfast after. Thankfully, AI only picks the teams. It still can't settle line calls.

The morning ended with the traditional group photograph — capturing what this tournament is really about: a growing community brought together by a shared love of the game.

A special thank you to Rahul Gupta, whose vision and passion are the driving force behind this tournament. From booking the courts and coordinating players to ensuring everyone is ready on time, Rahul quietly manages everything behind the scenes and has created a wonderful tradition that the community now eagerly looks forward to every month. 🙏

Here's to more tennis, more friendships, more laughter, and another exciting Mini Doubles Tournament next month. See you on the clay courts! 🎾
